# CUZY: HumHub modules and hosting

CUZY Hosting is managed SaaS hosting of [HumHub](https://github.com/humhub/humhub), the open-source intranet and community platform, by HumHub specialists: CUZY has built HumHub modules since 2020 (34 on the marketplace) and contributes to the core, so support means fixing the code, not forwarding a ticket. Fully managed: updates applied for you after a canary phase, security fixes within 48 hours, encrypted backups, monitoring. Custom modules and themes for your community on Business plan, written by your developers or by CUZY. 100% open source and exportable: move to your own server at any time. A flat price with unlimited members (Team €90 a month, Business €450) in the region you choose: Germany (EU), the USA or Singapore (APAC). 14-day free trial, no card.
