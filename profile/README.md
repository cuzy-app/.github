HumHub specialists: CUZY has built HumHub modules since 2020 and contributes to the core, so support means fixing the code, not forwarding a ticket.

Fully managed: updates applied for you after a canary phase, security fixes within 48 hours, encrypted backups, monitoring.

Custom modules and themes for your community on Business plan, written by your developers or by CUZY.
